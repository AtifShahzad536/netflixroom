Zero-Drift Playback Sync

Smart frame-accurate synchronization (<300ms drift tolerance) with automated loop-prevention flags. When the host plays, pauses, or seeks, everyone's Netflix responds instantly.

WebRTC Spatial Voice & Noise Isolation

Cascaded 24dB/octave high-pass filter (260 Hz) completely eliminates laptop mechanical vibrations and keyboard clicks while amplifying crystal-clear human vocal formants.

Rich Animated Stickers & Emojis

1-Click Quick Reaction bar, categorized searchable emojis, and high-impact watch party badges designed for hype movie moments.

Chrome Side Panel Experience

Operates cleanly in Chrome's dedicated Side Panel API. Never closes when clicking outside, keeping your chat, presence, and voice controls always visible.

Master & Individual Peer Volumes

Control the total party volume or adjust each friend's audio level individually so loud friends never overpower quiet conversations.

Push-to-Talk (Spacebar) Mode

Switch between AI Auto Voice Gate and Push-to-Talk (hold Spacebar) mode for 100% dead silence when snacking or typing heavily.
